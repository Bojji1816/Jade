# Bail Design Concepts for M003 / M017 / M018

Purpose: explore minimal, non-traditional pendant bails that still have a distinct design point and match each jade piece's colour.

## M003

Stone: 厚實甜甜圈，灰白底帶墨綠/黑花。

Recommended metal direction: oxidized silver, gunmetal, brushed sterling silver, black cord.

Concepts:
- Black bridge bail: flat oxidized silver bridge through the top, strongest masculine / architectural feeling.
- Brushed silver tension tabs: two asymmetric clips, clean and modern, keeps the hole visible.
- Gunmetal crescent bail: off-centre crescent hugging the donut edge, most distinctive and best matches the smoky pattern.

## M017

Stone: 白底橙花平安扣。

Recommended metal direction: champagne gold, rose gold, soft yellow gold.

Concepts:
- Champagne saddle bail: low rounded top cap, simplest and easiest to produce.
- Rose-gold dot-and-line bail: small warm accent echoes the orange flowers, more memorable.
- Folded ribbon bail: sculptural but still simple; slightly more conventional than the other two.

Image: `M017-bail-concepts.png`

## M018

Stone: 糯冰飄綠平安扣，淡青白底帶局部深綠飄花。

Recommended metal direction: white gold, matte silver, tiny mint green accent.

Concepts:
- Floating bar bail: very minimal, cleanest for icy green jade.
- Split-loop bail: two fine parallel arcs, light and modern.
- Mint enamel block bail: silver base with pale green accent, most distinctive while still restrained.
