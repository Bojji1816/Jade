## Usage Rules

- Use the primary logo on warm white or another quiet, low-contrast background.
- Keep the horizon line and west-side half-sun in their approved positions.
- Keep `JADE | 巧翠` centered below the horizon line.
- Use the three material shapes together and in this order: pale jade oval,
  lavender rounded square, honey-jade soft triangle.
- Upload the square Instagram file directly. Instagram applies its own circular
  crop; do not add a circle, border, card, or shadow to the source image.
- Do not stretch, condense, recolor, outline, or apply effects to the wordmark.
- Keep clear space around the lockup at least equal to the height of the letter
  `A` in `JADE`.
