# AFTERGLOW JADE Identity Audit

Date: 2026-08-19
Status: Selected direction; refinement pending
Evidence: `01-selected-identity.png`

## Overall verdict

The Quiet Dawn direction is strong enough to become the main identity. The serif wordmark and the thin horizon/half-sun device give AFTERGLOW a clear visual story without relying on literal sunset imagery. The next round should simplify the system and make it reliable at small sizes and on textured materials.

## Step 1: Primary lockup

Health: Strong, with production refinements needed.

- Keep the uppercase serif wordmark, horizon line, and half-sun motif.
- Reduce the half-sun diameter slightly so it reads as a quiet detail rather than a second logo.
- Create a small-size wordmark with slightly thicker hairlines and tighter spacing; the current high-contrast strokes may break on fabric, foil, embossing, and small screens.
- Refine the Chinese typography for `巧翠`; the current supporting face feels more utilitarian than the English wordmark.

## Step 2: Color and material system

Health: Promising but overextended.

- Keep near-black, warm white, pale jade, and warm amber as the core palette.
- Keep muted lavender as a secondary jade color.
- Remove vermilion from the core palette unless a real application is defined; it currently adds noise without supporting the afterglow idea.
- Do not use pale jade, lavender, or amber for small text on white. Their contrast is too weak for reliable reading; use near-black or mineral green for text and reserve pale colors for shapes and surfaces.
- Rename consumer-facing `MATERIAL 01` and `MATERIAL 02`. For a jade-led brand, use meaningful groups such as `PALE JADE`, `LAVENDER JADE`, and `HONEY JADE`, or use product categories instead.

## Step 3: IG and small applications

Health: Needs simplification.

- Keep the full `AFTERGLOW` name in the IG profile image.
- Use only `AFTERGLOW` plus `JADE | 巧翠` in the avatar; remove the three material chips at profile-picture size.
- Keep the `A` monogram as a secondary stamp for tiny labels, seals, and hardware, not as the main IG identity.
- Define three official lockups: full brand lockup, wordmark-only, and small monogram.

## Step 4: Website, cards, and packaging

Health: Good visual fit, with practical risks.

- Replace the heavy black band behind the product photography with warm stone, soft grey, or natural paper so the shop feels consistent with the identity.
- Use an optical print version of the wordmark on the cotton pouch; the current hairlines are too fragile on textured fabric.
- Keep `A quiet glow that stays.` as supporting copy, not part of the mandatory logo.
- Limit the three colored shapes to navigation, collection coding, stickers, and editorial layouts. They should not appear under every logo application.

## Recommended refinement scope

1. Redraw the primary and small-size wordmarks.
2. Refine the `JADE | 巧翠` typography and spacing.
3. Simplify the IG avatar.
4. Reduce the palette to core and secondary roles.
5. Rename the three material categories.
6. Produce one-color, foil/emboss, fabric-print, and digital master versions.

## Evidence limits

This review is based on one generated identity board. Actual legibility, foil detail, embroidery, emboss depth, color accuracy, and fabric print quality still require physical or vendor proofs.
